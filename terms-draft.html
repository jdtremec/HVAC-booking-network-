Customer Booking Terms

This platform connects customers with independent HVAC and plumbing companies. It does not perform or supervise repair work. A request is not an appointment until an identified provider accepts and the customer authorizes the provider, visit price and terms. The provider is responsible for technical diagnosis, permits, workmanship and any applicable warranty. Additional repairs, parts, taxes or fees require a separate quote and consent. No real payments are accepted on this pilot website. Cancellation charges, if any, must be disclosed before acceptance. Contact the booking coordinator if an appointment fails or a complaint needs assistance. These terms are a draft pending Mississippi counsel review.
