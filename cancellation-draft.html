Cancellation and Rescheduling

Before customer approval

Customers may withdraw an unconfirmed request without a platform cancellation fee.

After confirmation

Any cancellation, late-arrival or no-access charge must be disclosed before authorization and comply with applicable law and the individual provider agreement.

Provider cancellations

We will attempt to coordinate a replacement where one is available; we cannot promise a fixed response time before network capacity is established.

Changes

The policy applicable to a booking must be retained with its customer consent record. Terms require counsel approval before launch.
